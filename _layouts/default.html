Who we are

Bahria University Animal Welfare Society

BUAWS is a student-driven society at Bahria University's E-8 Islamabad campus, operating under the Student Resource Centre. We're dedicated to fostering compassion, providing animal care, and raising awareness about animal rights across the twin cities.

From organizing the beloved "Critter's Carnival" pet fest, to educational initiatives and community service drives — our members show up for animals who cannot speak for themselves.

This adoption portal is our way of making sure every animal we document gets a real shot at a forever home.
